Company

Built on field experience since 1995.

Shubhangi Mining Services, formerly Shubhangi Marketing Services, is a mining contractor based in Udaipur with experience across iron ore, limestone, masonry stone, sandstone and allied mineral operations.

The company undertakes mining, crushing, screening and associated mineral operations across multiple project locations in India.

Vision

To build a dependable, professionally managed mining services company known for disciplined execution and long-term relationships.

Mission

To deliver reliable mining and mineral operations through strong field execution, capable teams and continuously improving systems.
